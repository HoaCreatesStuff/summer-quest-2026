Install Summer Quest

Summer Quest works best when saved to your phone like a real app.

It only takes about 10 seconds.

  1. Step 1

    Tap the Share button on the bottom of your screen.

  2. Step 2

    Tap "See more", scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".

  3. Step 3

    Tap "Add" and make sure you enable "Open as Web App".

  1. Step 1

    Tap the browser menu (⋮) on the top right of your screen.

  2. Step 2

    Tap "Add to Home Screen", "Install App", or "Add Page to" depending on your browser.

  3. Step 3

    Tap "Add" to save it to your phone.

You’ll only need to do this once.
